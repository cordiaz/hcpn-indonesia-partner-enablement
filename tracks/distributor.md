# HCPN Indonesia — Distributor Track

> **Track Focus:** Channel Management, Partner Ecosystem Development, Partner Operations, and Business Activation  
> **6-Month Outcome:** **Channel Ready**  
> **12-Month Outcome:** **Ecosystem-Orchestrating, Partner-Activating, Business-Generating Distributor**

---

## 1. Track Purpose

The Distributor Track is designed to develop Huawei Cloud distributor capability from basic HCPN ecosystem understanding into measurable downstream partner recruitment, enablement, activation, and business growth.

The intended progression is:

**HCPN Understanding → Partner Operations → Channel Management → Partner Enablement → Partner Activation → Ecosystem Growth**

This track should not be treated as a sequence of training events only. Every month should produce a measurable operational, partner-management, or business-enablement outcome.

---

## 3. Entry Requirements

No advanced technical background is required, but participants should understand basic cloud business concepts and channel operations.

Recommended minimum readiness:

- understanding of basic cloud services;
- familiarity with partner/channel sales models;
- basic knowledge of distributor operations;
- awareness of partner lifecycle concepts;
- registered participation in the HCPN enablement program.

Optional pre-assessment:

- channel maturity assessment;
- partner portfolio review;
- current downstream partner segmentation;
- partner activation baseline;
- partner business capability survey.

---

## 4. 6-Month Capability Objective

By the end of Month 6, a distributor should be able to:

1. explain the HCPN ecosystem and distributor role;
2. operate relevant partner onboarding and management processes;
3. understand partner lifecycle and partner segmentation;
4. recruit and classify potential downstream partners;
5. create a partner enablement plan;
6. track downstream partner capability and activation;
7. support partner sales and solution development;
8. build joint business development activities;
9. manage a basic partner pipeline and activation dashboard;
10. pass the Distributor Channel Capability Assessment.

# 5. Six-Month Learning Roadmap

## Month 1 — HCPN Distributor Foundation

### Theme

**Foundation**

### Objective

Build a common understanding of Huawei Cloud, HCPN, the distributor role, and how the distributor contributes to ecosystem scale.

### Core Topics

- Huawei Cloud overview
- HCPN ecosystem overview
- Distributor role and responsibilities
- Partner types and engagement model
- Reseller, Service Partner, and CSP overview
- Partner lifecycle
- Channel business model
- Partner recruitment fundamentals
- Partner enablement fundamentals
- Distribution operational model
- High-level cloud portfolio awareness
- Partner business outcome model

### Learning Outcomes

Participants should be able to:

- explain the role of a distributor in HCPN;
- identify major downstream partner types;
- explain how distributors expand Huawei Cloud market reach;
- understand the partner lifecycle;
- differentiate partner recruitment from partner activation.

### Recommended Delivery

| Activity | Duration |
|---|---:|
| Instructor-Led Training | 3 hours |
| Ecosystem Workshop | 2 hours |
| Self Learning | 2 hours |
| Assessment | 30 minutes |

### Practical Assignment

Create a **Current Partner Ecosystem Map**.

Example structure:

| Partner | Partner Type | Current Capability | Current Status | Potential |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Partner A | Reseller | Sales | Active | High |
| Partner B | Service Partner | Technical | Developing | Medium |
| Partner C | CSP | Solution | Not Activated | High |

### Evidence

Distributor may submit:

- ecosystem mapping sheet;
- training assessment result;
- public-safe partner segmentation summary.

### Completion Gate

Month 1 is considered complete when:

- [ ] foundation training completed;
- [ ] ecosystem map created;
- [ ] distributor role understood;
- [ ] knowledge assessment completed;
- [ ] partner baseline documented.

# 10. Month 6 — Distributor Channel Capability Assessment

### Theme

**Validation**

### Objective

Validate that the distributor can manage the downstream partner lifecycle from recruitment to business activation.

### Assessment Components

| Assessment | Weight |
|---|---:|
| HCPN / Ecosystem Knowledge | 15% |
| Partner Operations | 15% |
| Partner Segmentation | 15% |
| Channel Management | 20% |
| Enablement Planning | 15% |
| Partner Business Activation | 20% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

### Final Simulation

Scenario:

> The distributor has a portfolio of downstream partners with different levels of sales, technical, and business capability. Some partners are active, some are newly recruited, and others have not generated business activity.

The distributor must:

1. segment the partner portfolio;
2. identify capability gaps;
3. prioritize partners;
4. recommend enablement tracks;
5. create an activation plan;
6. define KPI and review cadence;
7. present the channel growth plan.

### Suggested Readiness Levels

| Score | Status |
|---|---|
| < 50 | Foundation |
| 50–69 | Developing |
| 70–84 | Channel Ready |
| 85–100 | Advanced Channel Ready |

> These thresholds are proposed program-management values and are not official Huawei Cloud/HCPN qualification thresholds.

### Month 6 Exit Criteria

A distributor becomes **Channel Ready** when:

- [ ] mandatory modules completed;
- [ ] Month 1–5 assignments completed;
- [ ] partner segmentation exists;
- [ ] activation plan exists;
- [ ] final channel capability assessment completed;
- [ ] readiness score meets the program target;
- [ ] next 90-day ecosystem growth plan exists.

# 11. Monthly Delivery Model

Each month follows the same enablement cycle:

## Week 1 — Learn

Instructor-led session or online learning.

## Week 2 — Practice

Workshop, platform exercise, case study, or channel simulation.

## Week 3 — Apply

Partner portfolio assignment or real downstream partner activity.

## Week 4 — Validate

Assessment, review, or capability validation.

The operating model is:

**Learn → Practice → Apply → Validate**

# 19. Definition of Success

The Distributor Track should not be reported only as:

> "30 distributor staff completed training."

A stronger management report is:

```text
30 distributor personnel trained
        ↓
25 channel-ready personnel
        ↓
80 downstream partners mapped
        ↓
50 partners prioritized
        ↓
35 partners enabled
        ↓
25 partners activated
        ↓
15 partner-led opportunities
        ↓
customer adoption / business outcome
```

This creates a measurable connection between distributor enablement and ecosystem productivity.
