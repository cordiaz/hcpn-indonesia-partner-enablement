# HCPN Indonesia — Partner Readiness Framework

> **Purpose:** Provide a consistent, evidence-based way to measure partner capability progression across Reseller, Distributor, Service Partner, and CSP tracks.

This framework is a **program-management model** for the HCPN Indonesia Partner Enablement repository. It is not an official Huawei Cloud partner qualification, certification, tiering, or incentive policy.

The intended progression is:

**Training → Knowledge → Capability → Readiness → Activation → Customer Adoption → Business Outcome**

---

## 1. Why Partner Readiness Matters

Training completion alone does not prove that a partner is ready to sell, manage, implement, or integrate Huawei Cloud solutions.

The Partner Readiness Framework adds a structured assessment layer between learning activities and business activation.

It answers five management questions:

1. Has the partner completed the required learning path?
2. Has the partner demonstrated the expected skills?
3. Is there sufficient evidence of capability?
4. Is the partner ready to move into customer-facing activity?
5. Is capability translating into activation and business outcomes?

---

## 2. Readiness Dimensions

The proposed model uses seven dimensions.

| Dimension | Weight | What It Measures |
|---|---:|---|
| Training Completion | 10% | Completion of required enablement activities |
| Knowledge Assessment | 15% | Understanding of products, solutions, policies, or technical concepts |
| Certification / Credential Readiness | 20% | Relevant certification progress or readiness |
| Hands-on / Assignment | 20% | Ability to apply learning through practical work |
| Partner Activation | 15% | Movement from training into real partner activity |
| Customer Opportunity / PoC | 10% | Evidence of customer-facing engagement |
| Business Outcome | 10% | Early measurable business impact |
| **Total** | **100%** | |

These weights are proposed for program management and may be adjusted by track owner or program governance.

---

## 3. Readiness Levels

| Score | Level | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **0–49** | Foundation | Learning has started, but capability is not yet sufficient for independent customer activity |
| **50–69** | Developing | Core knowledge exists, but capability still requires guidance or additional validation |
| **70–84** | Ready | Partner demonstrates sufficient capability for the expected role and may proceed to customer activation |
| **85–100** | Advanced | Partner demonstrates strong capability, evidence, and activation maturity |

Recommended public status values:

- `Foundation`
- `Developing`
- `Ready`
- `Advanced`

---

## 4. Dimension 1 — Training Completion

### Weight

**10%**

### Measures

- mandatory module completion;
- attendance;
- monthly learning activities;
- required workshops;
- required self-learning.

### Example Scoring

| Completion | Score |
|---|---:|
| < 50% | 20 |
| 50–69% | 50 |
| 70–89% | 75 |
| 90–99% | 90 |
| 100% | 100 |

### Evidence

- attendance record;
- LMS completion record;
- workshop participation;
- monthly progress checklist.

### Public-Safe Data

Allowed:

- completion status;
- percentage completion;
- current month / stage.

Avoid publishing personal learner data without approval.

---

## 5. Dimension 2 — Knowledge Assessment

### Weight

**15%**

### Measures

- quiz results;
- pre-test / post-test improvement;
- product understanding;
- architecture or policy understanding;
- role-specific knowledge.

### Example Scoring

Use a normalized score from 0 to 100.

Example:

```text
Assessment Result: 82/100
Readiness Dimension Score: 82
Weighted Contribution: 82 × 15% = 12.3
```

### Evidence

- assessment score;
- assessor feedback;
- pre/post-test result;
- role-play review.

### Public-Safe Data

Recommended public representation:

```text
Knowledge Assessment: Validated
```

instead of exposing personal scores where not necessary.

---

## 6. Dimension 3 — Certification / Credential Readiness

### Weight

**20%**

### Measures

Depending on track:

- certification completed;
- certification preparation completed;
- exam readiness;
- required credential achieved;
- relevant technical or sales competency evidence.

### Suggested Scoring

| Status | Score |
|---|---:|
| Not Started | 0 |
| Learning Started | 25 |
| Exam / Assessment Ready | 60 |
| Certification / Credential Completed | 100 |

### Evidence

- certification status;
- exam readiness review;
- credential confirmation;
- approved program record.

### Privacy Rule

Do not publish:

- exam voucher codes;
- certification IDs;
- candidate IDs;
- private exam results;
- personal account information.

Public status may simply show:

```text
Certification Status: Completed
```

---

## 7. Dimension 4 — Hands-on / Assignment

### Weight

**20%**

This dimension is critical because readiness should demonstrate application, not only knowledge.

### Possible Evidence by Track

#### Reseller

- customer discovery exercise;
- solution pitch;
- proposal;
- account plan.

#### Distributor

- partner recruitment plan;
- partner segmentation;
- channel activation plan;
- enablement plan.

#### Service Partner

- architecture;
- migration plan;
- deployment lab;
- runbook;
- troubleshooting exercise;
- capstone implementation.

#### CSP

- packaged solution;
- architecture;
- integration design;
- demo;
- PoC design;
- GTM plan.

### Suggested Assessment Criteria

| Criterion | Weight within Assignment |
|---|---:|
| Completeness | 20% |
| Technical / Business Accuracy | 30% |
| Practical Applicability | 25% |
| Communication / Documentation | 15% |
| Review Feedback Closure | 10% |

---

## 8. Dimension 5 — Partner Activation

### Weight

**15%**

Partner Activation means the partner has moved beyond learning and has started performing its intended ecosystem role.

### Activation by Partner Type

| Track | Activation Example |
|---|---|
| Reseller | Starts customer discovery, account planning, or pipeline activity |
| Distributor | Recruits, enables, or activates downstream partners |
| Service Partner | Participates in implementation, migration, or technical delivery |
| CSP | Packages, demonstrates, or positions an integrated solution |

### Suggested Scoring

| Status | Score |
|---|---:|
| No activation | 0 |
| Activation plan exists | 30 |
| Initial activity started | 60 |
| Active and validated | 100 |

### Evidence

- activation plan;
- public-safe activity summary;
- approved internal CRM reference;
- partner review outcome.

---

## 9. Dimension 6 — Customer Opportunity / PoC

### Weight

**10%**

### Measures

- customer workshop;
- qualified opportunity;
- discovery session;
- PoC candidate;
- active PoC;
- solution demonstration.

### Suggested Scoring

| Activity | Score |
|---|---:|
| No customer activity | 0 |
| Customer engagement identified | 30 |
| Workshop / discovery completed | 60 |
| Qualified opportunity / PoC active | 80 |
| PoC validated | 100 |

### Public Repository Rule

Do not publish:

- customer name without approval;
- opportunity value;
- customer architecture;
- pricing;
- contract details;
- internal CRM identifiers.

Recommended public representation:

```text
Customer Engagement: In Progress
PoC Status: Active
```

---

## 10. Dimension 7 — Business Outcome

### Weight

**10%**

This dimension connects enablement with measurable business impact.

Possible indicators:

- opportunity progression;
- new customer acquisition;
- downstream partner activation;
- production deployment;
- cloud consumption;
- renewal / upsell;
- partner-attributed revenue.

### Important

Business metrics are typically confidential.

The public repository should normally use qualitative status only.

Example:

```text
Business Outcome: Validated
```

Detailed financial data should remain in internal reporting systems.

---

# 11. Readiness Score Formula

The total score is calculated as:

```text
Partner Readiness Score =
(Training Completion × 10%) +
(Knowledge Assessment × 15%) +
(Certification Readiness × 20%) +
(Hands-on / Assignment × 20%) +
(Partner Activation × 15%) +
(Customer Opportunity / PoC × 10%) +
(Business Outcome × 10%)
```

Example:

| Dimension | Score | Weight | Contribution |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Training Completion | 100 | 10% | 10.0 |
| Knowledge Assessment | 85 | 15% | 12.75 |
| Certification | 80 | 20% | 16.0 |
| Hands-on / Assignment | 90 | 20% | 18.0 |
| Partner Activation | 70 | 15% | 10.5 |
| Customer Opportunity / PoC | 60 | 10% | 6.0 |
| Business Outcome | 50 | 10% | 5.0 |
| **Total** | | | **78.25** |

Result:

```text
Readiness Level: Ready
```

# 12. Track-Specific Interpretation

The same score should be interpreted according to partner role.

## Reseller

**Ready** means the partner can:

- conduct customer discovery;
- position Huawei Cloud solutions;
- prepare basic customer proposals;
- initiate pipeline activity;
- perform customer-facing sales activity with appropriate support.

### Target Outcome

**Sales Ready**

---

## Distributor

**Ready** means the partner can:

- operate partner onboarding / administration processes;
- recruit and segment downstream partners;
- deliver partner enablement;
- create activation plans;
- monitor downstream partner activity.

### Target Outcome

**Channel Ready**

---

## Service Partner

**Ready** means the partner can:

- design an appropriate cloud architecture;
- perform implementation or migration activities;
- operate and troubleshoot solutions;
- produce runbooks and delivery documentation;
- complete a validated technical delivery scenario.

### Target Outcome

**Technical Delivery Ready**

---

## CSP

**Ready** means the partner can:

- design integrated solutions;
- combine Huawei Cloud with partner / third-party services;
- package solutions;
- demonstrate solutions;
- prepare PoC and go-to-market activities.

### Target Outcome

**Solution Ready**

# 13. Readiness Gates by Program Phase

## Month 1 — Foundation Gate

Partner demonstrates basic understanding of its role and Huawei Cloud fundamentals.

Recommended minimum:

```text
Readiness Level: Foundation
```

---

## Month 2 — Knowledge Gate

Partner demonstrates sufficient role-specific knowledge.

Recommended expectation:

- training completion progressing;
- knowledge assessment completed;
- core concepts understood.

---

## Month 3 — Capability Gate

Partner starts demonstrating practical capability.

Recommended expectation:

- assignments completed;
- role-play / lab evidence available;
- capability gaps identified.

---

## Month 4 — Practice Gate

Partner applies capability through realistic scenarios.

Recommended expectation:

```text
Readiness Level: Developing or above
```

---

## Month 5 — Activation Gate

Partner begins role-specific ecosystem activity.

Examples:

- Reseller → sales activation;
- Distributor → downstream partner activation;
- Service Partner → technical project readiness;
- CSP → solution packaging / demo readiness.

---

## Month 6 — Readiness Validation Gate

Partner completes final assessment.

Recommended target:

```text
Readiness Score >= 70
Readiness Level = Ready
```

This threshold is a proposed program-management value, not an official HCPN qualification threshold.

---

# 14. Month 7–12 Advanced Readiness

The second half of the program should increase the quality of evidence rather than simply repeat training.

Focus areas:

- advanced capability;
- specialization;
- industry knowledge;
- customer workshops;
- PoC;
- production deployment;
- business impact.

Recommended advanced target:

```text
Readiness Level: Advanced
```

where sufficient evidence exists.

---

# 15. Evidence Quality Model

Not all evidence has equal strength.

Suggested evidence levels:

| Level | Evidence Type | Example |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Self-declared | Checklist update |
| E2 | Documented | Assignment / training record |
| E3 | Reviewed | Assessor-approved deliverable |
| E4 | Demonstrated | Demo, lab, pitch, workshop, implementation |
| E5 | Business Validated | Customer / partner activation or production outcome |

For a partner to reach **Ready**, important capability dimensions should ideally contain at least E3 or E4 evidence.

For **Advanced**, E4 and E5 evidence should be present where applicable.

# 19. Readiness Review Checklist

Before changing a partner to `Ready`:

- [ ] mandatory training is complete;
- [ ] knowledge assessment is complete;
- [ ] required certification / credential status is verified;
- [ ] role-specific assignment is reviewed;
- [ ] hands-on evidence is available;
- [ ] partner activation has started;
- [ ] readiness score is calculated;
- [ ] track owner validates the result;
- [ ] public information has been checked for confidentiality.

Before changing to `Advanced`:

- [ ] advanced / specialization training completed;
- [ ] customer-facing activity demonstrated;
- [ ] PoC / production or equivalent role-based activation exists;
- [ ] higher-quality evidence is available;
- [ ] business outcome has been reviewed where applicable.

# 22. Management Reporting View

Instead of reporting only:

```text
500 participants trained
```

management reporting should evolve toward:

```text
500 participants trained
        ↓
420 completed
        ↓
320 validated
        ↓
220 partner personnel Ready
        ↓
120 partner activations
        ↓
60 customer engagements
        ↓
30 PoCs
        ↓
production / business outcomes
```

This provides a stronger connection between learning investment and ecosystem capability.
