# HCPN Indonesia — Partner Capability Funnel

> **Purpose:** Define how partner training progresses into measurable capability, activation, customer adoption, and business outcomes.

The HCPN Indonesia Partner Enablement Program should not be measured only by the number of training sessions or participants.

The core measurement model is:

```text
Reach
  ↓
Registration
  ↓
Attendance
  ↓
Learning
  ↓
Certification
  ↓
Capability
  ↓
Partner Activation
  ↓
PoC / Solution Engagement
  ↓
Production Deployment
  ↓
Customer Adoption
  ↓
Revenue / Business Outcome
```

This funnel connects enablement activity with actual partner and customer impact.

---

## 1. Why the Capability Funnel Matters

Traditional training reporting often stops at:

```text
Training
  ↓
Attendance
  ↓
Certificate
```

That model shows activity, but does not prove whether the partner can:

- sell Huawei Cloud solutions;
- manage downstream partners;
- implement and operate workloads;
- integrate and package solutions;
- create opportunities;
- run customer workshops;
- execute PoCs;
- move workloads into production;
- contribute to cloud consumption or revenue.

The capability funnel therefore extends training measurement into business enablement.

---

## 2. Funnel Stages

## Stage 1 — Reach

### Definition

The number of target partners or partner personnel exposed to the enablement opportunity.

### Typical Activities

- training invitation;
- partner communication;
- campaign announcement;
- partner manager outreach;
- event promotion.

### Example KPIs

- invited partners;
- invited participants;
- invitation coverage;
- partner segment coverage.

### Example Formula

```text
Reach Coverage = Invited Target Partners / Total Target Partners × 100%
```

### Public Reporting

Safe to publish aggregated counts where appropriate.

---

## Stage 2 — Registration

### Definition

Partners or participants who formally register for the program or training activity.

### Example KPIs

- registrations;
- registration rate;
- registration by partner type;
- registration by geography;
- registration by role.

### Example Formula

```text
Registration Rate = Registered Participants / Invited Participants × 100%
```

### Interpretation

Low registration may indicate:

- low perceived relevance;
- scheduling conflict;
- weak partner communication;
- wrong target audience;
- insufficient business context.

---

## Stage 3 — Attendance

### Definition

Participants who actually attend the planned enablement activity.

### Example KPIs

- attendance count;
- attendance rate;
- no-show rate;
- attendance by track;
- attendance by partner.

### Example Formula

```text
Attendance Rate = Attended Participants / Registered Participants × 100%
```

### Interpretation

Attendance is an operational indicator, not a capability indicator.

High attendance does not automatically mean the partner is ready.

---

## Stage 4 — Learning

### Definition

Evidence that participants acquired knowledge or completed the required learning activities.

### Example KPIs

- module completion;
- learning hours;
- pre-test score;
- post-test score;
- assessment completion;
- hands-on lab completion;
- learning improvement.

### Example Formula

```text
Learning Improvement = Post-Test Score - Pre-Test Score
```

or

```text
Learning Improvement % = (Post-Test - Pre-Test) / Pre-Test × 100%
```

### Evidence

Possible evidence includes:

- assessment result;
- completed lab;
- submitted assignment;
- role-play outcome;
- workshop deliverable.

---

## Stage 5 — Certification

### Definition

Participants who achieve or progress toward relevant Huawei Cloud certification or formal competency validation.

### Example KPIs

- certification candidates;
- certified personnel;
- certification pass rate;
- certification readiness;
- certification level distribution.

### Example Formula

```text
Certification Pass Rate = Passed Candidates / Exam Candidates × 100%
```

### Important Note

Certification validates knowledge and competency but should not be treated as the final program outcome.

The next question must be:

> Can the partner use this capability in a real customer or ecosystem context?

---

## Stage 6 — Capability

### Definition

The partner demonstrates role-specific ability, not only training completion.

Capability differs by partner track.

| Partner Track | Capability Outcome |
|---|---|
| Reseller | Sales Ready |
| Distributor | Channel Ready |
| Service Partner | Technical Delivery Ready |
| CSP | Solution Ready |

### Examples by Track

#### Reseller

- perform discovery;
- map requirements to solutions;
- present a proposal;
- conduct solution selling.

#### Distributor

- onboard partners;
- manage partner lifecycle;
- build enablement plans;
- activate downstream partners.

#### Service Partner

- design architecture;
- perform migration;
- implement workloads;
- operate and support services.

#### CSP

- integrate services;
- build packaged solutions;
- deliver demos;
- prepare PoC and GTM plans.

### Example KPIs

- Partner Readiness Score;
- validated personnel;
- completed capstone;
- assignment quality;
- architecture review result;
- proposal quality;
- sales pitch score.

---

## Stage 7 — Partner Activation

### Definition

The partner starts applying the capability in actual business or ecosystem activity.

### Examples

#### Reseller

- starts customer discovery;
- creates account plans;
- develops qualified opportunities.

#### Distributor

- recruits downstream partners;
- activates partner enablement plans;
- runs channel programs.

#### Service Partner

- joins implementation opportunities;
- supports migration workshops;
- starts customer delivery activity.

#### CSP

- packages a solution;
- runs demos;
- develops customer use cases.

### Example KPIs

- activated partners;
- active partner ratio;
- solution workshops;
- account plans;
- partner campaigns;
- active technical engagements.

### Example Formula

```text
Partner Activation Rate = Activated Partners / Capability-Ready Partners × 100%
```

---

## Stage 8 — PoC / Solution Engagement

### Definition

Partner capability is applied to a concrete customer or solution validation activity.

### Typical Activities

- customer workshop;
- solution assessment;
- architecture workshop;
- demo;
- technical validation;
- proof of concept;
- migration assessment.

### Example KPIs

- customer workshops;
- PoCs initiated;
- PoCs completed;
- demo sessions;
- solution assessments;
- PoC conversion rate.

### Example Formula

```text
Training-to-PoC Conversion = Partners with PoC Activity / Capability-Ready Partners × 100%
```

---

## Stage 9 — Production Deployment

### Definition

The solution or workload moves from evaluation into a real production environment.

### Example KPIs

- production deployments;
- migration projects completed;
- implemented solutions;
- implementation success rate;
- time to production;
- production workload count.

### Example Formula

```text
PoC-to-Production Conversion = Production Deployments / Completed PoCs × 100%
```

### Interpretation

This is one of the strongest indicators that enablement has translated into real technical or commercial value.

---

## Stage 10 — Customer Adoption

### Definition

Customers actively consume or expand the deployed cloud solution.

### Example KPIs

- active customers;
- workload adoption;
- repeat workloads;
- service expansion;
- cloud consumption growth;
- customer satisfaction.

### Example Indicators

- additional services adopted;
- production workload growth;
- repeat engagement;
- renewal;
- expansion into new workloads.

---

## Stage 11 — Revenue / Business Outcome

### Definition

The final business impact attributable, directly or indirectly, to partner enablement.

### Example KPIs

- partner-generated pipeline;
- partner-attributed revenue;
- cloud consumption;
- customer acquisition;
- renewal;
- upsell;
- time-to-market;
- Return on Training Investment (ROTI).

### Example Formula

```text
ROTI = Business Benefit Attributed to Training / Training Investment
```

### Important Note

Revenue attribution can be complex and should be treated carefully.

Training is usually one contributing factor among many, including:

- sales engagement;
- marketing;
- customer budget;
- solution competitiveness;
- partner capability;
- pricing;
- delivery quality;
- market timing.

For that reason, reporting should distinguish between:

- **training-correlated outcome**;
- **partner-attributed outcome**;
- **directly attributable outcome**, where evidence exists.

# 8. Public vs Internal Metrics

Because this repository is public, not every metric should be published.

## Recommended Public Metrics

Safe examples:

- training status;
- track;
- current month;
- completion status;
- validated capability level;
- public-safe certification status;
- number of completed activities;
- generic PoC status where approved.

## Recommended Internal Metrics

Keep these in internal systems unless formally approved for public disclosure:

- customer name;
- opportunity value;
- pipeline amount;
- pricing;
- revenue;
- cloud consumption amount;
- contract data;
- confidential partner performance;
- personal certification identifiers;
- internal Huawei metrics.

A public repository may show:

```text
PoC Status: In Progress
```

instead of:

```text
Customer: <confidential customer>
Opportunity Value: <confidential value>
```

# 11. Funnel Health Indicators

A healthy funnel should not only have a large top-of-funnel.

Example warning patterns:

## High Reach, Low Registration

Possible issue:

- low relevance;
- poor communication;
- wrong audience.

## High Attendance, Low Learning

Possible issue:

- ineffective content;
- insufficient hands-on activity;
- weak assessment alignment.

## High Certification, Low Activation

Possible issue:

- certification is not linked to partner business execution;
- no customer opportunity;
- weak account planning;
- missing post-training follow-up.

## High PoC, Low Production

Possible issue:

- solution gaps;
- pricing;
- implementation risk;
- customer readiness;
- delivery capability.

These signals help program owners decide where intervention is required.

# 12. Executive Reporting Model

Instead of reporting:

> 1,000 people participated in training.

A stronger executive report is:

```text
1,000 Participants Reached
        ↓
800 Registered
        ↓
700 Attended
        ↓
600 Completed
        ↓
450 Certified / Validated
        ↓
300 Capability Ready
        ↓
180 Partner Activated
        ↓
90 Customer Engagements / PoCs
        ↓
40 Production Deployments
        ↓
Customer Adoption / Business Outcome
```

This gives management visibility into where capability is created and where conversion is lost.

---

# 13. Definition of Success

The capability funnel is successful when the program can demonstrate a measurable relationship between:

**Training → Skill → Certification → Capability → Activation → Customer Engagement → Production → Adoption → Business Outcome**

The objective is to build measurable ecosystem enablement, not only training volume.
