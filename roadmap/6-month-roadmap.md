# HCPN Indonesia — 6-Month Partner Enablement Roadmap

> **Program Scope:** Reseller, Distributor, Service Partner, and Cloud Solution Provider (CSP)  
> **Program Duration:** 6 Months  
> **Program Model:** **Learn → Practice → Apply → Validate**

---

## 1. Program Purpose

This roadmap provides a common six-month enablement structure for HCPN partners in Indonesia.

The objective is not only to deliver training, but to progressively build measurable partner capability that can lead to certification readiness, partner activation, customer engagement, and business outcomes.

The intended enablement journey is:

**Training → Capability → Certification → Partner Activation → Customer Adoption → Revenue**

Each partner type follows the same six-month maturity pattern, but with different role-specific outcomes.

## 3. Six-Month Program Logic

The six-month program uses a common capability progression:

```text
M1 — Foundation
      ↓
M2 — Knowledge
      ↓
M3 — Capability Development
      ↓
M4 — Practice
      ↓
M5 — Activation
      ↓
M6 — Validation
```

The management question for each month is:

| Month | Management Question |
|---|---|
| M1 | Does the partner understand Huawei Cloud and its role? |
| M2 | Does the partner understand the knowledge required for its role? |
| M3 | Can the partner apply that knowledge to a realistic scenario? |
| M4 | Can the partner practice the role in a structured workshop or lab? |
| M5 | Can the partner convert capability into partner or customer activity? |
| M6 | Can the partner demonstrate measurable readiness? |

# 11. Recommended Monthly Delivery Cycle

Each month follows a common operating rhythm.

## Week 1 — Learn

Instructor-led training, webinar, or guided learning.

## Week 2 — Practice

Workshop, lab, demo, role-play, or scenario exercise.

## Week 3 — Apply

Partner completes a role-specific assignment or practical task.

## Week 4 — Validate

Assessment, review, certification readiness, or capability validation.

```text
Learn
  ↓
Practice
  ↓
Apply
  ↓
Validate
```

This prevents the program from becoming a series of disconnected training events.

---

# 12. Recommended Effort Model

The exact duration can be adjusted by track, but a typical monthly cycle can use:

| Activity | Suggested Effort |
|---|---:|
| Instructor-Led / Guided Learning | 3–4 hours |
| Workshop / Lab | 2–4 hours |
| Self Learning | 2–4 hours |
| Assignment | 2–4 hours |
| Assessment / Review | 0.5–2 hours |

For Service Partner, hands-on time should normally be higher than other tracks.

# 14. KPI Framework

The program should be measured across multiple layers.

## Layer 1 — Training Operations

- registration rate;
- attendance rate;
- completion rate;
- drop-off rate;
- learning hours;
- training capacity.

## Layer 2 — Learning

- assessment score;
- pre/post-test improvement;
- module completion;
- certification readiness;
- certification pass rate where applicable.

## Layer 3 — Partner Capability

- partner readiness score;
- role-specific capability score;
- certified personnel;
- validated personnel;
- capability gap closure.

## Layer 4 — Partner Activation

Examples differ by track:

### Reseller

- account plans;
- discovery meetings;
- qualified opportunities;
- solution workshops.

### Distributor

- recruited downstream partners;
- trained partners;
- activated partners;
- partner campaigns.

### Service Partner

- PoCs;
- implementation projects;
- migration projects;
- successful technical assessments.

### CSP

- packaged solutions;
- demos;
- PoCs;
- GTM initiatives.

## Layer 5 — Business Impact

Where approved and appropriate:

- pipeline;
- customer acquisition;
- production workloads;
- cloud consumption;
- partner-generated revenue;
- ROTI / training ROI.

Confidential business information should remain outside the public repository.

# 15. Partner Readiness Model

A proposed common readiness model can be used across tracks:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Training Completion | 10% |
| Knowledge Assessment | 15% |
| Certification | 20% |
| Hands-on / Assignment | 20% |
| Partner Activation | 15% |
| Customer Opportunity / PoC | 10% |
| Business Outcome | 10% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

Suggested capability levels:

| Score | Level |
|---|---|
| `< 50` | Foundation |
| `50–69` | Developing |
| `70–84` | Ready |
| `85–100` | Advanced |

> These values are proposed program-management thresholds and are not official Huawei Cloud or HCPN qualification criteria.

# 17. Transition to the 12-Month Program

Month 6 is not the end of the enablement journey.

The second six months should move from foundational capability into specialization and measurable market activation.

```text
M1–M6
Foundation → Capability → Validation

                 ↓

M7–M12
Advanced Capability → Specialization → Customer Engagement → PoC → GTM → Business Review
```

The second half may include:

- advanced product capability;
- AI specialization;
- Data specialization;
- Cloud Native specialization;
- Security specialization;
- industry enablement;
- customer workshops;
- PoC execution;
- joint GTM;
- annual business review.

# 19. Public Repository Rules

This repository is intended for public progress visibility.

Do **not** publish:

- Huawei confidential information;
- customer confidential information;
- opportunity value;
- confidential pricing;
- partner contractual information;
- credentials or API keys;
- exam voucher codes;
- personal certification identifiers;
- proprietary course materials without redistribution rights.

Public progress should focus on:

- current stage;
- capability status;
- validated milestone;
- public-safe evidence;
- next milestone.

# 20. Definition of Program Success

The program should not be reported only as:

> “X training sessions delivered and Y participants attended.”

A stronger enablement report is:

```text
Partners Reached
      ↓
Participants Trained
      ↓
Participants Completed
      ↓
Participants Validated / Certified
      ↓
Partners Ready
      ↓
Partners Activated
      ↓
Customer Engagement
      ↓
PoC / Opportunity
      ↓
Production Adoption
      ↓
Business Outcome
```

The six-month roadmap therefore acts as a **Partner Capability Development System**, not only a training calendar.
