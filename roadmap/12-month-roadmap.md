# HCPN Indonesia — 12-Month Partner Enablement Roadmap

> **Program Scope:** Reseller, Distributor, Service Partner, and Cloud Solution Provider (CSP)  
> **Program Goal:** Build partner capability, validate readiness, develop specialization, activate customer engagement, and connect enablement to business outcomes.

---

## 1. Purpose

This roadmap extends the first six months of HCPN Indonesia partner enablement into a full 12-month capability and business activation journey.

The program is designed around the following progression:

**Training → Capability → Certification → Partner Activation → Customer Engagement → PoC → Production → Adoption → Revenue**

The first six months focus on foundational capability and readiness. The second six months focus on advanced capability, specialization, customer engagement, and measurable business activation.

# 5. Months 1–6 — Core Enablement Phase

The first half of the program builds role-based capability.

## Month 1 — Foundation

### Objective

Establish common understanding of Huawei Cloud, HCPN partner role, and track-specific fundamentals.

### Track Outcomes

- **Reseller:** understand product portfolio and customer value proposition.
- **Distributor:** understand channel role and partner ecosystem.
- **Service Partner:** understand core Huawei Cloud technical services.
- **CSP:** understand cloud-native and solution integration fundamentals.

---

## Month 2 — Knowledge

### Objective

Develop enough technical, operational, or architectural understanding to support the partner role.

### Track Outcomes

- **Reseller:** conduct credible technical sales conversations.
- **Distributor:** operate partner lifecycle and partner administration.
- **Service Partner:** design foundational cloud architecture.
- **CSP:** create basic integrated solution architecture.

---

## Month 3 — Capability Development

### Objective

Move from understanding into role-specific execution capability.

### Track Outcomes

- **Reseller:** perform discovery and solution selling.
- **Distributor:** manage HCPN ecosystem and partner enablement.
- **Service Partner:** build migration and infrastructure modernization capability.
- **CSP:** develop AI, data, and integrated solution capability.

---

## Month 4 — Practice

### Objective

Validate knowledge through workshop, lab, role-play, architecture, or commercial practice.

### Track Outcomes

- **Reseller:** prepare customer-facing commercial proposal.
- **Distributor:** build channel management and partner segmentation approach.
- **Service Partner:** demonstrate cloud-native, data, and modernization skills.
- **CSP:** build or refine a cloud-native solution package.

---

## Month 5 — Activation

### Objective

Translate capability into practical partner activity.

### Track Outcomes

- **Reseller:** create a sales activation plan.
- **Distributor:** create a downstream partner activation plan.
- **Service Partner:** prepare for advanced competency and certification.
- **CSP:** build pre-sales and solution positioning capability.

---

## Month 6 — Validation

### Objective

Assess whether each partner has reached the required readiness level for its role.

### 6-Month Exit Outcomes

| Track | Target Outcome |
|---|---|
| Reseller | **Sales Ready** |
| Distributor | **Channel Ready** |
| Service Partner | **Technical Delivery Ready** |
| CSP | **Solution Ready** |

---

# 14. Partner Readiness Model

A proposed annual readiness model can use the following dimensions:

| Dimension | Suggested Weight |
|---|---:|
| Training Completion | 10% |
| Knowledge Assessment | 10% |
| Certification / Credential Readiness | 15% |
| Hands-on / Assignment | 15% |
| Role Capability | 15% |
| Specialization | 10% |
| Partner Activation | 10% |
| Customer Engagement / PoC | 10% |
| Business Outcome | 5% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

Suggested maturity levels:

| Score | Level |
|---|---|
| `< 50` | Foundation |
| `50–69` | Developing |
| `70–84` | Ready |
| `85–100` | Advanced |

> These values are proposed program-management thresholds and are not official Huawei Cloud or HCPN qualification criteria.

# 17. Annual Success Criteria

The program should not be evaluated only by statements such as:

> "We delivered 30 training sessions and trained 1,000 participants."

A stronger annual report connects training to business outcomes:

```text
1,000 participants reached
        ↓
800 attended
        ↓
650 completed
        ↓
500 validated / certified-ready
        ↓
350 role-ready personnel
        ↓
200 specialized personnel
        ↓
120 partner activations
        ↓
80 customer engagements
        ↓
40 PoCs
        ↓
20 production deployments
        ↓
cloud adoption / business outcome
```

The exact values above are illustrative examples, not official targets.
